# Data availability

Synthetic inputs, analytic reference grids and historical outputs are included with the software. The field-data addition supplies the exact processed Olympic Dam gravity/elevation inputs and archived comparison grids. These are the numerical materials used by the manuscript; they were not regenerated for packaging.

The earlier-study source grids match the corresponding retained files of Zhang et al.(2025), DOI10.6038/cjg2024S0470, byte for byte. That publication identifies SARIG as the original Bouguer gravity source. See FIELD_DATA_PROVENANCE.md and provenance/field_source_trace.json for official access links, original-source attribution, file descriptions and hash evidence. The original portal-download version/date, historical datum/EPSG code and original DEM acquisition product are not established. No such metadata are fabricated or inferred from the current portal.
